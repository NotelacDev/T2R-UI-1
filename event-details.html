Event Overview

Join us for the highly anticipated Southern Stadium Run, a premier endurance event bringing together athletes from all over the country. Featuring official RFID chip timing by Time To Result Solutions, participants will run through an accurately measured route with comprehensive on-ground hydration and medical support.

Whether you are aiming for a personal best in the 10K or taking on the full marathon challenge, this event promises a world-class experience. Enjoy our vibrant BIB Expo, post-race festivities, and exclusive finisher entitlements including a custom zinc medal and dry-fit t-shirt.
